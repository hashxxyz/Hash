# Hamid Sahari Format Reference — Project Guidance

This document describes high-level format characteristics for our own football-animation system. It is not a library of Hamid Sahari scripts or artwork.

## Observed format principles

- Start from a recognizable real football event, result, transfer, controversy, meme, or player situation.
- Establish the premise quickly, often within the opening seconds.
- Focus on one clear comedic angle rather than a complicated multi-act story.
- Use exaggerated physical or visual comedy.
- Escalate through a small number of quick beats.
- Finish with a clear punchline or visual payoff.
- Short-form pacing is important; many examples are roughly in the 20–60 second range, though length varies.
- Characters are generally selected because they fit the event rather than because every character must have a permanent sitcom personality.
- Recognizable football situations and reactions are important.
- Simple environments and strong silhouettes make production efficient.

## Our interpretation

Use these as broad format lessons for our own original universe. Do not reproduce specific Hamid scripts, jokes, drawings, character designs, scenes, music, or shot sequences.

## Our target formula

REAL FOOTBALL EVENT
→ ONE ORIGINAL FUNNY ANGLE
→ VISUAL GAG
→ QUICK ESCALATION
→ PUNCHLINE

## Research note

When an event is current, verify it with reliable current sources before treating it as fact.
